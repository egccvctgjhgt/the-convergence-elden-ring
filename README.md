## Overview

The Convergence is one of Elden Ring's most comprehensive overhaul mods, adding 15+ new character classes with unique starting builds, hundreds of new spells, ashes of war, and weapons, a complete gesture overhaul, new NPC questlines, and rebalanced enemy AI and player stats. It represents months of community development to create an expanded Elden Ring experience that feels like a major DLC.

Whether you're a newcomer just getting started or a seasoned pro looking for advanced tools, **The Convergence** delivers the features you need in a clean, intuitive package.
